Part names

The names and functions of the parts of the main unit and the foot switch.

No. Name Function
1 Press bar Comes down and holds the bag. Silicone rubber is attached to the underside.
2 Sealing section The heater wire and the heat-resistant sheet that covers it. Heats the bag and welds it.
3 Work table The table on which you place the bag.
4 Control panel Sets the sealing temperature and time.
5 Power switch Turns the power of the machine on and off.
6 Power cord Connects to a grounded outlet.
7 Foot switch When pressed, performs one seal.
8 Photoelectric sensor In automatic mode, detects a bag placed at the sealing position.
