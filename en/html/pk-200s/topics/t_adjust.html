Adjusting the sealing temperature and time

Set the sealing temperature, heating time, and cooling time to suit the material and thickness of the bag.

The following settings are a guide. The thickness is the total thickness of the front and back of the bag.

Table 1. Guide to sealing conditions
Bag material and thickness Sealing temperature Heating time Cooling time
Polyethylene, 0.1 mm 120 °C 0.5 s 1.0 s
Polyethylene, 0.2 mm 130 °C 0.8 s 1.5 s
Polypropylene, 0.1 mm 150 °C 0.6 s 1.0 s
Laminated bag, 0.2 mm 170 °C 1.0 s 2.0 s
  1. Press the Temperature button.

    The sealing temperature on the display blinks.

  2. Press the Up button or the Down button to change the sealing temperature.

    You can set it from 80 °C to 200 °C in 5 °C steps.

  3. Press the Temperature button again to confirm.
  4. Press the Time button, and change the heating time with the Up button or the Down button.

    You can set it from 0.2 seconds to 2.0 seconds.

  5. Press the Time button again, and change the cooling time in the same way.

    You can set it from 0.5 seconds to 3.0 seconds.

  6. Press the Time button to confirm.

    The settings are saved even when the power is turned off.

Note:
The appropriate sealing conditions depend on the material and thickness of the bag. When you use a new type of bag, make a test seal and check the result.
