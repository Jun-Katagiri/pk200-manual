Troubleshooting

If the machine does not work properly, check the cause and the remedy for each symptom.

If an error code is shown on the display, see Error codes.

Note:
If the problem persists after you take the corrective action, stop using the machine and contact the dealer where you purchased it.

The power does not turn on

The power plug is disconnected from the outlet or is not fully inserted.

Turn off the power switch and insert the power plug all the way into the outlet.

The press bar does not come down when the foot switch is pressed

The foot switch plug is disconnected from the main unit.

Insert the foot switch plug all the way into the foot switch connector on the rear of the main unit.

The Auto lamp is lit and the foot switch does not work

The machine is in automatic mode. The foot switch does not work in automatic mode.

Press the Mode button to switch to manual mode.

The seal is weak and comes apart when pulled

The sealing temperature is too low or the heating time is too short.

Raise the sealing temperature in 5 °C steps or lengthen the heating time in 0.1 second steps, and make a test seal.

The seal is uneven

The heat-resistant sheet or the silicone rubber is dirty or damaged.

Wipe off the dirt. If the part is scorched, torn, or dented, replace it.

The sealed part melts and has holes or breaks

The sealing temperature is too high or the heating time is too long.

Lower the sealing temperature in 5 °C steps or shorten the heating time in 0.1 second steps, and make a test seal.

The sealed part is wrinkled

The cooling time is too short, and the press bar rises before the bag has set.

Lengthen the cooling time in 0.5 second steps, and make a test seal.

The press bar does not come down when a bag is inserted in automatic mode

The lens of the photoelectric sensor is dirty.

Turn off the power switch and wipe the lens with a soft, dry cloth.

Certain bags are not detected in automatic mode

The photoelectric sensor may not detect transparent bags or highly glossy bags.

Switch to manual mode and operate the machine with the foot switch.
