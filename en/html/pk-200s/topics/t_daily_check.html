Daily inspection

Every day, before you start operation, carry out the following inspection and cleaning.

Warning
Risk of electric shock.
Death or serious injury may result.
Before inspection, cleaning, or parts replacement, turn off the power switch and unplug the power cord from the outlet.
CAUTION
The sealing section becomes hot.
Touching it may cause burns.
Do not touch the heater wire or the area around it during operation or for 10 minutes after turning off the power.
  1. Check that the power cord and the power plug are not damaged or deformed.
  2. Check that the heat-resistant sheet is not scorched or torn.

    If it is scorched or torn, replace it with a new heat-resistant sheet.

  3. Check that the heater wire is not bent or slack.

    If you find a problem, replace it by following the procedure in Replacing the heater wire.

  4. Wipe off any dirt on the silicone rubber of the press bar with a soft, dry cloth.
  5. Check that the foot switch cable is not damaged or broken.
  6. Wipe the lens of the photoelectric sensor with a soft, dry cloth.

    If the lens is dirty, the machine may not detect bags in automatic mode.
