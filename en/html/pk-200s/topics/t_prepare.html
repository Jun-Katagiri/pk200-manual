Preparing for operation

Turn on the power and check the sealing conditions for the bags you will use.

Complete the daily inspection.

  1. Turn on the power switch and check that the buzzer sounds once.

    The display shows the current sealing temperature setting.

  2. Adjust the sealing temperature, heating time, and cooling time settings to suit the bags you will use.

    For how to change the settings, see Adjusting the sealing temperature and time.

  3. Make a test seal with the same type of bag that you will use.
  4. Gently pull the sealed part to the left and right, and check that it does not come apart.

If there is no problem with the seal, you can start operation.
