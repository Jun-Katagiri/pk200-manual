The main specifications of the PK-200.

Item Specifications
Model PK-200
Sealing method Impulse sealing (with temperature control)
Operating modes Manual (foot switch)
Power supply 100 V AC, 50/60 Hz
Power consumption 400 W
Seal length 200 mm max.

Sealing temperature 80 °C to 200 °C (in 5 °C steps)

Mass 12.5 kg
Operating environment Temperature: 5 °C to 35 °C, humidity: 35 % to 85 % (no condensation)
